
Explaining Multithreading in Java
Consumer producer problem
The Producer-Consumer Problem (also known as the Bounded-Buffer Problem) is a classic concurrency design pattern. It involves two types of threads sharing a fixed-size buffer:

Producer: Generates data and puts it into the buffer.

Consumer: Removes data from the buffer and processes it.

The Core Synchronization Challenges
Buffer Full: The producer must wait until space becomes available before adding more items.

Buffer Empty: The consumer must wait until items are added before removing any.

Race Conditions: Both threads must access the buffer safely without corrupting data or causing deadlocks.
